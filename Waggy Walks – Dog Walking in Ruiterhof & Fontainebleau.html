About Waggy Walks

Hi! I’m the 15-year-old behind Waggy Walks. I’m building my own small business while saving towards my goals, staying active, and doing something I genuinely enjoy.

I’ve got experience with dogs and understand how important it is for them to get exercise, attention, and care. I want every owner to feel confident that their dog is being looked after responsibly.

Waggy Walks is about happy dogs, reliable service, and giving local pet owners a helping hand. Every booking means a lot to me, and I aim to treat every dog with patience, care, and respect.

FAQ

Booking, cancellation & refund policy

Booking. All walks must be booked in advance. A booking is confirmed only when the website clearly states that it has been accepted.

Cancellation. Please give at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule whenever possible. Any late-cancellation fee must be disclosed and agreed before booking; none is currently charged.

Weather & safety. Waggy Walks may postpone or cancel a walk if weather, dog welfare or safety makes it unsuitable, and will offer rescheduling or a refund for prepaid walks not provided.

No-shows. If nobody is available at the agreed meeting point, I’ll try to contact you; the walk may then be treated as missed. Any charge will be explained to you first.

Payment. Payment methods are agreed on confirmation. No online payments are available on this site.

Draft terms – to be reviewed by a parent or guardian against South African consumer law before launch.
